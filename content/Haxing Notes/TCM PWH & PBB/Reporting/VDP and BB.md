>Vulnerability Reporting and Disclosure
	VDPs do not pay out and are generally broader focused, whereas Bug Bounty programs pay out and are more specific.

>Avoid the following problems:
1. Lack of information or step-by-step instructions
2. Send images or videos instead of raw text for the POC
3. Ignoring the scope and rules
4. Exaggerating the severity of a vulnerability
5. Not mentioning the impact of the vuln on the org's users
6. Sending multiple reports for same vuln
7. Submitting very low level/info level vulns (ex. typo in web page)
8. Not being courteous and being impatient
9. Leaking sensitive information
10. Having unrealistic payout expectations
11. Failing to VERIFY the vulnerability's impact i.e. in different contexts
